## 9. Data caveats

- Google Play tiers are rounded minimums ("1M+" = 1,000,000–4,999,999). Review counts vary by locale snapshot (TTS Pintar 598K–615K).
- AppBrain totals and 30-day installs are third-party estimates; several snapshots are stale (Vita Crossword ~512 d, TTS Pintar ~543 d, Zytoona ~175 d, IsCool ~386 d) and some pages show internal contradictions (Vita Solitaire 160K vs 95K/30d). Fresh (≤7 d) figures: Vita Solitaire, Vita Jigsaw, Sudoku.com, PL/AT word charts, Berni NL.
- AppBrain ranking tables' "Recent" column is treated as ≈30-day installs but is not labelled as such.
- play.google.com could not be fetched directly (page truncation) and appbrain.com returned 403; all figures come from search-result snippets of those pages.
- Language lists for CodyCross/Wordscapes/Crossword Jam come from App Store listings and may differ on Android.
- Review weaknesses are from a handful of surfaced reviews, not a systematic scrape.
